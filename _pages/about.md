I am an Associate Researcher in the iGEN lab at Mount Sinai, working on computational approaches to biomedical target discovery. My work includes multimodal modeling, perturbational data analysis, and reproducible machine learning workflows.

Previously, I worked on molecular graph learning at Regenerative Bio and studied liver vascular biology in the Pradhan-Sundd Lab at the University of Pittsburgh.

I hold an M.S. in Epidemiology from New York University (2025) and a B.S. in Microbiology from the University of Pittsburgh (2023). My master's thesis used NHANES data to examine associations between serum ferritin and cholesterol profiles in U.S. women.
